The astronomy planner is adapted from `smeird/weather`,
`frontend/astro/planner.php` (https://github.com/smeird/weather).

`moon.php` is the Weather repository's PHP moonrise/moonset calculation,
original mathematics and JavaScript by Keith Burnett, PHP port by Matt
"dxprog" Hackmann. It is distributed under GPL version 3 or later. Its
original copyright/licence notice is preserved; the licence text is in COPYING.

The phase/illumination estimate and 15-minute moon visibility sampling match
the Weather planner. These are approximate planning calculations, not telemetry.
